The new [ExternalNow], [ExternalSleep], [ExternalNewTimer], [ExternalAfter],
[ExternalAfterFunc], [ExternalNewTicker], and [ExternalTick] functions operate
on an external ("real time") monotonic clock that continues to advance while the
system is asleep, unlike the standard monotonic clock used by [Now] and the
regular timers, which measures "program time" and stops while the system is
asleep. On systems that do not provide a distinct sleep-surviving clock, these
functions behave exactly like their standard counterparts.
